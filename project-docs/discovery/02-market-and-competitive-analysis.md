# Market & Competitive Analysis

> Status: Draft · Phase 0 · Desk research to be validated with primary data.

## 1. Category
Online travel accommodation & short-term rental marketplaces, expanding toward broader "stays and spaces."

## 2. Reference players (studied for UX patterns, NOT to copy)
- **Airbnb** — marketplace mechanics, search-first home, listing detail, host onboarding funnel.
- **Booking.com** — dense search results, filters, price transparency, urgency cues.
- **Vrbo** — whole-home focus, family/group positioning.
- **Regional PropTech** (e.g., local African/emerging-market platforms) — payment-provider fit (Paystack/Flutterwave), trust/verification emphasis.

## 3. Pattern library (what good marketplaces do well)
Search as the homepage hero; sticky booking widget on detail; honest photo galleries with counts; transparent fee breakdown before commitment; reviews with structured ratings; map + list toggle; saved/favorites; host dashboards with earnings.

## 4. Differentiation for Havyn
1. **Whole-price honesty** — all-in totals shown early, computed and verified server-side.
2. **Trust infrastructure** — verification/KYC workflow, dependable refunds, dispute handling first-class.
3. **Premium, calm design** — editorial photography-led UI, no generic dashboard aesthetic.
4. **API-first** — one backend for web + mobile from day one.

## 5. Market risks
Two-sided cold-start (supply vs demand), trust/fraud, payment/settlement complexity across geographies, regulatory (short-let regulations, taxes, KYC/AML). Mitigations tracked in product/business-model and security docs.

## 6. Positioning statement
For travelers who care where they stay and hosts who take pride in their spaces, Havyn is the trust-first property marketplace that makes every booking honest and every stay feel like arrival — unlike opaque, fee-surprising incumbents.
