# Personas & Jobs-To-Be-Done

> Status: Draft · Phase 0.

## Persona 1 — Amara, the Intentional Traveler (Customer)
32, urban professional, books 4–6 trips/yr. **Job:** "Help me find a place I can trust, understand the true price up front, and book it in minutes." **Pains:** hidden fees, misleading photos, cancellation anxiety. **Success:** honest total, easy dates/guests, instant confirmation, easy messaging.

## Persona 2 — Daniel, the Proud Host (Host)
45, owns 1–3 properties. **Job:** "Let me list professionally, control my calendar and price, and get paid reliably without spreadsheets." **Pains:** clunky tools, unclear payouts, double-bookings, no-shows. **Success:** fast listing creation, calendar control, clear earnings/payouts, guest communication.

## Persona 3 — The First-Time Guest (Guest, unauth)
Browsing before committing to an account. **Job:** "Let me explore and see if this is worth signing up for." **Success:** full discovery without login; account required only to book/save/message.

## Persona 4 — Operations/Trust Admin (Admin)
**Job:** "Keep the marketplace safe, fair, and running — moderate listings, resolve disputes, verify hosts, watch analytics." **Success:** clear moderation queues, KYC workflow, dispute tooling, platform metrics.

## Core JTBD summary
| Actor | Primary job | Make-or-break moment |
|---|---|---|
| Traveler | Book a trusted stay at an honest price | Price transparency + confirmation |
| Host | List and earn reliably | Payout clarity + calendar control |
| Guest | Evaluate before committing | Frictionless discovery |
| Admin | Keep marketplace trustworthy | Moderation + dispute tooling |
